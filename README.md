# Spider-Man

**Spider-Man** is a beloved superhero movie franchise featuring *Peter Parker*, a teenager who gains spider-like abilities and struggles to balance everyday life with heroism.

---

## Why I love Spider-Man
- Great blend of action and heart
- Relatable protagonist
- Memorable supporting characters
- Strong soundtrack and visuals

---

### Favorite Moments
1. The first time Peter realizes his powers
2. The rooftop conversations
3. The big final confrontation

> "With great power comes great responsibility."
